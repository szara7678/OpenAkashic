# Frozen same-source new-question validation

Authored 2026-11-01 before validation candidate outputs. Only the nine pinned original source text files/manifest and author-held knowledge of the earlier authored prompts were used. No prior results, grades, implementation, or metrics were inspected for this authoring task. Nine new scenarios, three each for Streamlit 1.46.0, TanStack Query 5.90.5, and DuckDB 0.10.0. All prompts are English and under 1200 characters. This is same-source NEW-question validation, not a new-domain, new-source, or fully external holdout.

Only direct and source-prefetch arms are compared (18 total runs). The method variant remains in the first report and is outside this validation comparison. Fixed denominator is nine tasks per arm. Crashes, invalid JSON, missing answers/evidence, and retrieval failures remain failures in that denominator. Each task has three exact typed decisions, an actionable recommendation, and uncertainty. Gold contains per-claim semantic anchors, example continuous supporting spans, and recommendation/uncertainty 0-2 rubrics. Equivalent supportive source spans are allowed. No minimum number of sources is required. Literal matching is necessary evidence validation, not semantic support. Unknown workload performance in validation-duckdb-02 must be answered unknown without invented measurements.

Full success per task requires strict output JSON, all three exact typed decisions, recommendation 2/2, uncertainty 2/2, and semantically supportive valid source quotations covering all answerable claims. The unknown claim is judged by explicit scope-bounded abstention. Quote normalization allows whitespace and inline Markdown/backtick removal only; quotes must preserve a continuous source word sequence and may not paraphrase or stitch. Proposed steps and example measurements must not be promoted to executed production validation.

Acceptance requires source-prefetch at least 8/9 full successes and at least as many full successes as direct; source-prefetch tokens per successful task must be at least 30 percent lower than direct, and wall seconds per successful task at least 20 percent lower. Include all run tokens and wall time, including failed runs, in numerators; divide by full-success count, and mark undefined/fail if no successes. Include source-prefetch overhead in tokens/time rather than silently excluding it. Report raw totals, success counts, and ratios separately. No model calls or candidate outputs are authorized by this protocol authoring step.
